DEPARTMENT FOR ENVIRONMENT, FOOD AND RURAL AFFAIRS
LAND DRAINAGE ACT 1991
THE POWYSLAND INTERNAL DRAINAGE DISTRICT (ABOLITION) ORDER 2015
Notice is hereby given that Welsh Ministers and the Secretary of State for Environment,
Food and Rural Affairs in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 3(5)
of the Land Drainage Act 1991, propose to make an Order confirming a Scheme submitted
by Natural Resources Wales and the Environment Agency under section 3(1) of the Act.
The scheme makes provision for:
i) Abolition of the Powysland Internal Drainage District.
ii) Creation of a new Internal Drainage District for the Welsh land previously within
the Powysland Internal Drainage District.
iii) Provision that the Natural Resources Body for Wales shall be the drainage board
in respect of the new district.
iv) Matters supplemental thereto or consequential thereon.
A copy of the Order, together with a copy of the Scheme has been deposited at the
offices of the Environment Agency, Sapphire East, 550 Streetsbrook Road, Solihull
B91 1QT, and at the offices of the Natural Resources Body for Wales at Cambria House,
29 Newport Road, Cardiff CF24 0TP and at the offices of the Department for Environment,
Food and Rural Affairs, Area 3C Nobel House, 17 Smith Square, London SW1P 3JR, and
at the offices of the Welsh Government, Flood and Coastal Erosion Risk Management,
Cathays Park, Cardiff CF10 3NQ for public inspection during normal office working
hours for a period of thirty days after the date of publication of this Notice.
Copies of the draft Order may be obtained from the Department for Environment, Food
and Rural Affairs or the Welsh Government.
Any objection to the draft Order should be made in writing and sent to the Department
for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, and to the Welsh Government, at the above
addresses, within the period mentioned above, quoting reference number IDBO/03, to
be received by 13 March 2015.
