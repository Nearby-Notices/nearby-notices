WATER RESOURCES ACT 1991
NOTICE OF APPLICATION FOR A LICENCE TO ABSTRACT WATER
Take note that Tarmac Limited of Millfields Road, Ettingshall, Wolverhampton WV4 6JP, is applying to the Environment Agency Wales for a licence to abstract water from Afon Glaslyn and an on-site sump at National Grid Refs SH 5915 3920 and SH 5912 3915 at Minffordd Quarry, Penryndeudraeth, Porthmadog, Merionethshire LL48 6HP. The maximum abstraction rates from both abstraction points combined shall be: 24 cubic metres per hour, 144 cubic metres per day, 41,760 cubic metres per year, between 1 January and 31 December each year. The water will be used for mineral washing all year and also for dust suppression as and when necessary in dry periods. A copy of the application and any map, plans and other documents submitted with it may be inspected, free of charge, at all reasonable hours at Minffordd Quarry, Penryndeudraeth, between 17 March 2006 and 14 April 2006. Any person who wishes to make representations about the application should do so in writing to the Environment Agency Wales, Llwyn Brain, Ffordd Penlan, Parc Menai, Bangor, Gwynedd LL57 4DE, before the end of the said period, quoting the name of the applicant. S Lawrence, on behalf of Tarmac Limited 17 March 2006.
