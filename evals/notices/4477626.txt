ROAD TRAFFIC REGULATION ACT 1984 SECTIONS 14 AND 15 (AS AMENDED BY THE ROAD TRAFFIC (TEMPORARY RESTRICTIONS) ACT 1991)
THE BOROUGH COUNCIL OF DUDLEY
THE BOROUGH COUNCIL OF DUDLEY (TEMPORARY BUS STATION) (EDNAM ROAD, PRIORY ROAD, NEW STREET, TOWER STREET, DUDLEY) (TEMPORARY ROAD RESTRICTION AND TRAFFIC REGULATION) ORDER 2023
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council propose to make the above mentioned Order not less than 21 days from the date of this Notice because works are or are being proposed to be executed on or near the roads detailed in the table set out below (“the Table”) in connection with the temporary relocation of bus station Tower Street, Ednam Road, New Street and Priory Street, Dudley.
The effects of which will be to introduce various Temporary Traffic Regulation Orders as detailed in the attached Schedule and to suspend parts of : The Borough Council of Dudley (Prohibition and Restriction of Waiting and Loading and Parking Places,
Disabled Residents Parking Places and No Stopping on School Entrance Markings) (Consolidation Order) (Variation 2.0) Order 2021 detailed in the attached schedule. at such times and extent when prescribed traffic signs and carriageway marking are being displayed
The effects of the Order shall apply only during such times and to such extent from time to time indicated by traffic signs prescribed by the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2016.
The Council is satisfied that the execution of the works will take longer than eighteen months and the Council shall revoke this Order as soon as the works are completed.
Traffic will be diverted and controlled as directed on site by the use of traffic signs.
The Order shall come into force at 08:00 hours on Monday 27th November 2023.
Any queries on the temporary restrictions imposed, should be made on (01384) 815453.
