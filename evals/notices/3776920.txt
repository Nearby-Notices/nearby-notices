ROAD TRAFFIC REGULATION ACT 1984 SECTIONS 14 AND 15 (AS AMENDED BY THE ROAD TRAFFIC (TEMPORARY RESTRICTIONS) ACT 1991)
THE BOROUGH COUNCIL OF DUDLEY (MIDLAND METRO TRAM EXTENSION) (BRIERLEY HILL TOWN CENTRE) (TEMPORARY ROAD RESTRICTION AND TRAFFIC REGULATION) ORDER 2021
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council have made the above mentioned Order because works are or are being proposed to be executed on or near the roads detailed in the table set out below (“the Table”) in connection with the Metro extension scheme.
The effect of the Order is that for a temporary period there will be restrictions introduced or suspended in relation to the roads listed in the table below.
The effects of the Order shall apply only during such times and to such extent from time to time indicated by traffic signs prescribed by the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2016.
The Council is satisfied that the execution of the works will take longer than eighteen months and the Council shall revoke this Order as soon as the works are completed.
The following roads may be used for diverted traffic: Bank Street, Bradleymore Road, Church Street, Cottage Street, Dudley Road, High Street (B4180), High Street, Highgate Road, John Street, Level Street, Leys Road, Little Cottage Street, Merry Hill, Mill Street, Moor Street, Mount Pleasant, Pedmore Road, Station Road, Stourbridge Road, Talbot Street, The Boulevard, The Gateway, Venture Way, Waterfront Way.
The Order shall come into force on the 2nd April 2021
Any queries on this matter should be made to Jeremy Butler on 01384 815457.
