WORCESTERSHIRE COUNTY COUNCIL
SECTION 116 AND SCHEDULE 12 TO THE HIGHWAYS ACT 1980
NOTICE is hereby given that Worcestershire County Council intends to make an application to the magistrates’ court sitting at Dudley Magistrates Court, The Inhedge, Dudley, DY1 1RY on 18 September 2024 at 10:00 am for an order under s. 116 Highways Act 1980 that the highway in the district of Bromsgrove in the county of Worcestershire adjacent to Bromsgrove Road, including U20625, Green Lane, Romsley, as indicated on the plan(s) available for public inspection free of charge at all reasonable hours at Worcestershire County Council, Wildwood, Wildwood Drive, Worcester, WR5 2QT and is represented by the parts coloured red shall be stopped up.
Dated: 08 August 2024
Thomas Pollock, Head of Commercial Law (Legal & Governance)
