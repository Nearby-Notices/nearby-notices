THE PETROLEUM ACT 1998
TRENT JACKETS AND RISER SECTIONS DECOMMISSIONING PROGRAMMES
Perenco UK Limited has submitted, for the consideration of the Secretary of State for the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, draft Decommissioning Programmes (DPs) for the Trent jackets and riser sections, in accordance with the provisions of the Petroleum Act 1998. It is a requirement of the Act that interested parties be consulted on such decommissioning proposals.
T
he DPs detail the planned removal of the Trent production platform (TP) jacket and the Trent compression platform (TC) jacket. This includes the riser sections of pipelines PL253b, PL1220 and PL1221 on the TP jacket, and the riser sections of pipelines PL2162 and PL2163 on the TC jacket.
The Trent jackets are located in the Trent gas field (UKCS Block 43/24a) in the Southern North Sea, approximately 116km from Flamborough, Yorkshire, on the UK coastline. The coordinates for the jackets are WGS84 latitude 54° 17' 56.154" N, longitude 01° 39' 30.367" E for the TP; and WGS84 latitude 54° 17' 55.174" N, longitude 01° 39' 28.418" E for the TC.
The Trent installations are located within the boundary of the Southern North Sea Special Area of Conservation, designated for the harbour porpoise.
