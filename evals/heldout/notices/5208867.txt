Notice category: Water Notice type: Water Resources Publication date: 10 September 2026 Edition: The London Gazette Notice ID: 5208867 Notice code: 1901 Issue number: 65175 Page number: 17666 REMOVAL OF RESTRICTIONS ON WATER USE
SECTIONS 76 AND 76A–C OF THE WATER INDUSTRY ACT 1991 - TEMPORARY BAN ON WATER USE
Since 19th July 2026 temporary prohibitions on the use of water (with some exceptions) have
been in force in the Mid and South Ceredigion water resource zone.
We are pleased to announce that, from 11am on Friday 4 September 2026 , all of these restrictions are lifted.
We are very grateful to our customers for their cooperation in conserving supplies. We ask
customers to continue to show restraint and to use water responsibly to help secure future
supplies. More information can be found at dwrcymru.com.
