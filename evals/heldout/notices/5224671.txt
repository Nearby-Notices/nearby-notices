STATUTORY NOTICE OF APPLICATION FOR DROUGHT ORDER
SECTIONS 73 AND 74(2)(F) AND SCHEDULE 8 OF THE WATER RESOURCES ACT 1991
TAKE NOTICE that due to the threat of a serious water shortage in Mid and East Devon caused by an exceptional shortage of rain, South West Water Limited (“SWW”) of Peninsula House, Rydon Lane, Exeter, EX2 7HR, is applying to Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs for a Drought Order under Section 73(3) of the Water Resources Act 1991.
The proposed Drought Order, if granted, would authorise SWW to prohibit or restrict specified non essential use of water. The restrictions proposed are those prescribed by the Drought Direction 2011 and may include restrictions on activities such as watering plants on commercial premises, filling or maintaining certain non-domestic swimming pools and ponds, vehicle washing, cleaning of non-domestic buildings and premises (including windows), cleaning industrial plant, dust suppression and other non essential uses specified in the Drought Direction 2011.
The application relates to the area supplied by South West Water within the Wimbleball Water Resource Zone. This includes Exeter, Exmouth, Crediton, Sidmouth, Tiverton and surrounding areas across Mid and East Devon and a small part of Dorset, including Lyme Regis. A more detailed description of the area affected by the proposed Drought Order will be made available if the application is successful.
Anyone may inspect the proposals, including relevant maps and plans, free of charge, up to and including 13 October 2026 at:
• South West Water Head Office, Peninsula House, Rydon Lane, EX2 7HR - between 9am-5pm, Monday – Friday
• www.southwestwater.co.uk/ non-essential-use-ban
