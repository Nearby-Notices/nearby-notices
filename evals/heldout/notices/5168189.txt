Notice category: Planning Notice type: Town and Country Planning Publication date: 3 July 2026 Edition: The London Gazette Notice ID: 5168189 Notice code: 1601 Issue number: 65116 Page number: 13245 DEPARTMENT FOR TRANSPORT
TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING ACT 1990
The Secretary of State hereby gives notice of the proposal to make an Order under section 247 of the above Act to authorise the stopping up of a length and part width of Cemetery Lane and a triangular shaped northern part width of Cemetery Lane at Wymondham in South Norfolk.
If the order is made, the stopping up will be authorised only to enable development as permitted by South Norfolk Council, under reference 2020/1439.
Copies of the draft order and relevant plan will be available for inspection during normal opening hours at Wymondham Library, Back Lane, Wymondham, NR18 0QB in the 28 days commencing on 03 July 2026, and may be obtained, free of charge, from the Secretary of State (quoting NATTRAN/E/S247/5054) at the address stated below.
Any person may object to the making of the proposed order by stating their reasons in writing to the Secretary of State at nationalcasework@dft.gov.uk or National Transport Casework Team, PO Box 1393, Newcastle Upon Tyne, NE99 5FQ, quoting the above reference. Objections should be received by midnight on 31 July 2026 . You are advised that your personal data and correspondence will be passed to the applicant/agent to enable your objection to be considered. If you do not wish your personal data to be forwarded, please state your reasons when submitting your objection.
S Zamenzadeh , Casework Manager
