Notice category: Environment Notice type: Environmental Protection Publication date: 25 September 2026 Edition: The Edinburgh Gazette Notice ID: 5216274 Notice code: 1803 Issue number: 29326 Page number: 1649 THE TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING (ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT) (SCOTLAND) REGULATIONS 2017
NOTICE UNDER REGULATIONS 21 AND 27
PROPOSED DEVELOPMENT AT: LAND AT INCHGARTH ROAD, PITFODELS, CULTS, ABERDEEN APPLICATION REFERENCE NUMBER 251313/DPP
Notice is hereby given that additional information has been provided to the planning authority through the environmental statement that has been submitted to Aberdeen City Council by Cala Management Ltd relating to the planning application in respect of the residential development of 100 units, including development of link road and road bridge, engineering works, associated infrastructure, open space and landscaping on 4 December 2025.
A copy of the environmental statement, the associated planning application and other documents submitted with the application may be inspected at all reasonable hours in the register of planning applications kept by the planning authority for the area at Aberdeen City Council, Strategic Place Planning, Business Hub 4, Marischal College, Broad Street, Aberdeen, AB10 1AB, and online on the Council’s website http://planning.aberdeencity.gov.uk/planningsearch.asp during the period of 30 days beginning with the date of this notice.
Copies of the environmental statement may be purchased from Ryden LLP, The Capitol, 431 Union Street, Aberdeen, AB11 6DA
PDF of Non-Technical Summary - This can be made available free of charge from Ryden LLP.
Hard Copy Environmental Statement (Main Volume & Appendices) - At a cost of £100 from Ryden LLP
USB containing a PDF version of the Main Volume & Appendices - At a cost of £25 from Ryden LLP
Any person who wishes to make representations to Aberdeen City Council about the Environmental Statement should make them in writing within the period of 30 days beginning with the date of this notice, to Aberdeen City Council, Strategic Place Planning, Business Hub 4, Marischal College, Broad Street, Aberdeen, AB10 1AB.
Possible decisions relating to the application are:
1. approval of the application without conditions;
2. approval of the application with conditions; and
3. refusal of the application.
