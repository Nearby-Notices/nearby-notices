Notice category: Planning Notice type: Town and Country Planning Publication date: 16 October 2026 Edition: The London Gazette Notice ID: 5232197 Notice code: 1601 Issue number: 65206 Page number: 20223 NATIONAL HIGHWAYS LIMITED
ROAD TRAFFIC REGULATION ACT 1984
THE A47 TRUNK ROAD (JAY LANE JUNCTION TO RACKHAMS CORNER, SUFFOLK) (CLOSURE OF GAPS IN THE CENTRAL RESERVATION) ORDER 202_
National Highways Limited( a ) gives notice that it proposes to make an Order under sections 1(1) and 2(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 on a length of the A47 Trunk Road at Lowestoft in Suffolk.
The effect of the Order would be to permanently close the gaps in the central reservation of the A47 Trunk Road located opposite the junctions with –
• Jay Lane,
• Access to Boyton Farmhouse,
• Access to Midway Nurseries
• Market Lane, and
• Stirrups Lane.
A copy of the proposed Order, together with a location plan and statement explaining National Highways Limited’s reasons for proposing to make the Order, may be inspected during normal office hours, between 16 October 2026 and 6 November 2026 at Lowestoft Library, Suffolk Libraries IPS Limited, Clapham Road South, Lowestoft, NR32 1DR and Oulton Broad Library, Bridge Road, Oulton Broad, Suffolk, NR32 3LR, or obtained by application to National Highways, Operations East, Orders Team, Priory Court, Priory Business Park, Stannard Way, Bedford, MK44 3WG.
Any person wishing to object to the proposed Order should write to National Highways Limited at the above address stating the reasons for the objection no later than 6 November 2026 or within 3 weeks of this publication, whichever is the latest.
Information provided in response to this notice, including personal information, may be subject to publication or disclosure in accordance with the access to information regimes (these are primarily the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (FOIA), the Data Protection Act 1998 (DPA) and the Environmental Information Regulations 2004.
