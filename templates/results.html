How the map works

Pins that would sit on top of each other are spread in a ring around their shared point, each joined to it by a short line. Zoom in to separate them.

Each pin sits at the nearest postcode the notice is filed under. For a council order that is often the council's address rather than the street affected; the notice itself names the places. The circle is your chosen distance. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, so opening this view means your browser requests them from OpenStreetMap's servers. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
